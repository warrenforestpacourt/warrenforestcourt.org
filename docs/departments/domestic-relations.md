# Domestic Relations Office of the 37th Judicial District

The Domestic Relations Office handles matters pertaining to child support,
spousal support and alimony _pendente lite_. Contact the office for
assistance in such matters. The office in Warren serves both Warren and
Forest Counties.

## Location

The Domestic Relations Office is located in the Hickory Street Annex at 333
Hickory Street, in the city of Warren. Get [directions](index.md#directions-hickory-street)
to the office. The main entrance to the office is off the parking lot on the
left side of the building, towards the back.
