# Court Departments

The Court departments of Warren County are housed in the Warren County
Courthouse, the Hickory Street Annex, North Warren and Youngsville. The Court
departments of Forest County are housed in the Forest County Courthouse and
Marienville. Please see the page for each department for more information.

/// details | Directions to the Warren County Courthouse
    type: directions
    attrs: 
        id: directions-warren-courthouse
        name: directions

The Warren County Courthouse is located on the corner of Fourth Avenue and
Market Street in the City of Warren (204 Fourth Avenue).

If approaching Warren from the North, Route 62 South becomes Market Street
which will intersect with Fourth Avenue.

If approaching Warren from the East, follow Route 6 Business West
(Pennsylvania Avenue), turn left on Market Street and travel North three
blocks to the intersection with Fourth Avenue.

If approaching Warren from the South (Route 62 North) or from the West(Route 6
East), follow Route 62 North to the intersection with Market Street.

Parking is available in the lots to the left (accessible from Fourth Avenue)
and rear (accessible from Fifth Avenue) of the Courthouse.

The Warren County Prison is located adjacent to the Courthouse at 407 Market
Street.

Courthouse Hours: 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM Monday – Friday

/// details | Directions to the Hickory Street Annex
    type: directions
    attrs: 
        id: directions-hickory-street

The Hickory Street Annex is located at 333 Hickory Street in the City of Warren.

From the front of the Courthouse (corner of Fourth and Market) take Fourth
Avenue east (Route 62 South). Turn right at Hickory Street (second traffic
light). The Hickory Street Annex is the third building on the right.

Parking is available in the small lot to the left of the building or on the street.

Office Hours: 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM Monday – Friday

    type: directions
    attrs: 
        id: directions-north-warren

The Magisterial District 37-3-01 Office is located at 4234 Market Street in
North Warren, Pennsylvania.

From the front of the Courthouse (corner of Fourth and Market) take Market
Street North (Route 62 North). The District Justice Office will be on the
right in approximately 3.8 miles. The Office is located in _Professionals
Plaza_, which is in front of _Tractor Supply_.

Office Hours: 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM Monday – Friday

/// details | Directions to the Youngsville Office
    type: directions
    attrs: 
        id: directions-youngsville
        name: directions

The Magisterial District 37-4-01 Office is located at 37 Railroad Street in
Youngsville, Pennsylvania.

If approaching Youngsville from the West (Route 6 East) follow Route 6 East to
the traffic light at the intersection with Railroad Street. Turn left onto
Railroad Street. The District Justice Office will be on the right.

If approaching Youngsville from the East (Route 6 West), North (Route 62
South), or South (Route 62 North), follow Route 6 West to the traffic light
at the intersection with Railroad Street. Turn right onto Railroad Street.
The District Justice Office will be on the right.

Office Hours: 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM Monday – Friday

[:lucide-map: View a map](http://maps.google.com/maps?hl=en&output=html&q=37+Railroad+Street%2C+Youngsville+PA+16371){ .md-button .md-button--secondary target='_blank' }

/// details | Directions to the Forest County Courthouse
    type: directions
    attrs: 
        id: directions-forest-courthouse
        name: directions

The Forest County Courthouse is located on the corner of Elm and May Streets
at 526 Elm Street in Tionesta, Pennsylvania.

If approaching from the Southeast, follow Route 36 North to Route 62 North
(Elm Street) and proceed one block to May Street.

From other directions approach is by Route 62 (Elm Street).

Parking is available in the lot behind the Courthouse which may be accessed
from May Street. There is additional parking on adjacent streets.

Courthouse hours: 9:00 AM – 12:00 Noon & 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM Monday, Tuesday,
and Thursday

The Magisterial District 37-4-02 Office is located at 271 Oak Lane in
Marienville, Pennsylvania.

If approaching Marienville from Route 66 South turn left onto Oak Lane. The
office will be on the right.

If approaching Marienville from Route 66 North turn right onto Oak Lane. The
office will be on the right.

Office hours: 9:00 AM – 12:00 Noon & 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM Monday, Wednesday, and
Friday
