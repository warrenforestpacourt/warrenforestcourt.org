*   ### Complaint for Custody &mdash; Grandparents and Third Parties

    Use this form to file for custody of children of which you are not a
    parent. You should use this form only if you are filing a new action.
    Instructions are included. Contact the [Family Hearing Office](/departments/family-hearing-office.md "Family Hearing Office") for details.

    [Download Form](/documents/custody/Custody_Complaint_Grandparents.pdf "Complaint for Custody &mdash; Grandparents and Third Parties"){ .md-button .md-button--secondary target="_blank" }

*   ### Complaint for Custody &mdash; Parents

    Use this form to file for custody of your children as a parent. You should
    use this form only if you are filing a new action. Instructions are
    included. Contact the [Family Hearing Office](/departments/family-hearing-office.md "Family Hearing Office") for details.

    [Download Form](/documents/custody/Custody_Complaint_Parents.pdf "Complaint for Custody &mdash; Parents"){ .md-button .md-button--secondary target="_blank" }

*   ### Petition for Modification of a Custody Order

    Use this form if you would like to modify (make changes to) your Court Order for
    custody. Instructions are included. NOTE: Does not apply to custody
    ordered through a PFA. Contact the [Family Hearing Office](/departments/family-hearing-office.md "Family Hearing Office") for details.

    [Download Form](/documents/custody/Modification_Petition.pdf "Petition to Modify Custody"){ .md-button .md-button--secondary target="_blank" }

*   ### Custody Contempt Petition

    Use this form to file a contempt petition against the other party in a
    custody matter for their failure to follow the Court Order. Instructions
    are included. NOTE: Does not apply to custody ordered through a PFA.
    Contact the [Family Hearing Office](/departments/family-hearing-office.md "Family
    Hearing Office") for details.

    [Download Form](/documents/custody/CustodyContemptPetition.pdf "Custody Contempt Petition"){ .md-button .md-button--secondary target="_blank" }

*   ### Criminal Record/Abuse History Verification

    Use this form to update your criminal record and/or abuse history with the Court.

    [Download Form](/documents/custody/Criminal_Record_Abuse_History-fillable-confidential-2025-06.pdf "Criminal Record/Abuse History Verification"){ .md-button .md-button--secondary target="_blank" }

*   ### Entry of Appearance of Self-Represented Party

    Use this form to enter your appearance as a self-represented party and inform the Court and other parties in your case of the mailing address at which you should receive all legal papers.

    [Download Form](/documents/custody/EntryOfAppearance.pdf "Entry of Appearance of Self-Represented Party"){ .md-button .md-button--secondary target="_blank" }

*   ### Petition to Proceed _In Forma Pauperis_ (Request Waiver of Filing Fee)

    Use this form to request a waiver of the filing fee. Submit this form
    along with your Complaint for Custody. Contact the [Family Hearing
    Office](/departments/family-hearing-office.md "Family Hearing Office") for details.

    [Download Form](/documents/custody/IFP.pdf "Petition to Proceed In Forma Pauperis (Request Waiver of Filing Fee)"){ .md-button .md-button--secondary target="_blank" }

*   ### Relocation

    Use these forms if you intend to relocate (move) with the child/children
    in a manner which significantly impairs the ability of a non-relocating
    party to exercise his/her custodial rights. Instructions are included.

    [Download Form](/documents/custody/Relocation.pdf "Relocation"){ .md-button .md-button--secondary target="_blank" }
